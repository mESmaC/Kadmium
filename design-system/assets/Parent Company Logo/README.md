# Parent Company Logo

YY (YinYangAI, yycore.ai) is Kadmium's parent brand. `Direction A in context@2x.png` is YY's own mark in three lockups — on a light tile, two-tone (off-white and YY's green) on a charcoal tile, and knocked out of a white disc — as uploaded for reference.

It is here so the family resemblance can be checked, not for use on Kadmium surfaces: Kadmium borrows the rounded-square tile format (22 % corner radius), the round-capped stroke language and the one-accent-on-charcoal palette structure, and nothing else. YY's interlaced strokes and green never appear in Kadmium, and the two marks are never combined inside one tile. For co-branding, set the two wordmarks side by side with one rod pitch of clear space between.
