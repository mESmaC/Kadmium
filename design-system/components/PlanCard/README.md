# PlanCard

A product on the pricing page: the free, self-hosted Kadmium or Kadmium Cloud, told apart by the product colour code and nothing else. Always show the two side by side at the same size, free first.

You provide `product` (`self-hosted` or `cloud`), `name`, `price` as text, `period`, a one-sentence `summary`, four or five `features` and a `cta`; `onSelect` handles the click. The eyebrow and feature bullets are rods in the product colour; the button is `primary` for self-hosted and `cloud` for cloud. No ribbons, no "most popular", no struck-through features on the free card.
